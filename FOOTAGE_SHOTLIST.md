# Legend — Demo Footage Shot List

Raw clips to film for the showcase demo. These are the **proof clips you upload** on the
*Proof of Legend* screen — the app's FFmpeg + HyperFrames pipeline auto-edits them into the
reel, then the AI grader scores how well they match the quest brief.

## Why these three quests

Picked as the **easiest to film and the safest to grade well** — none require a stranger's
face or staged action, so you can shoot them solo on a phone in one outing.

| Quest | Proof the app asks for | Grades on |
|---|---|---|
| **Chase the Light** (sunrise) | "Photograph the sky at its best." | A clearly beautiful sky |
| **End of the Line** (laststop) | "Photograph wherever the line leaves you." | Any evocative arrival/place |
| **Plant What Outlives You** (tree) | "Photograph the tree the day you planted it." | A visible sapling + soil/hands |

## How the app uses your clips (shoot for this)

- **Shoot 3–5 short clips per quest, 4–8s each.** The editor keeps the audible moments and
  concatenates them — more short clips = better pacing than one long static take.
- **Keep natural ambient sound on** (wind, birds, footsteps, a spade in soil). Silence gets
  trimmed; ambient audio survives and improves the cut.
- **Hold the phone steady** (brace against something or use a small tripod). The grader's
  "visual quality" sub-score punishes shake and blur.
- **Vertical or horizontal both work** — the engine normalizes non-H.264 sources. Pick one
  orientation and stay consistent within a quest.
- **Expose for the subject**, not the bright sky (tap-to-focus on your phone), except for the
  sunrise quest where the sky *is* the subject.

## 1. Chase the Light — sunrise / sunset 🌅

**Brief:** "Watch a full sunrise or sunset, with no screen between you and it."
**Proof:** "Photograph the sky at its best."

Easiest of the three. Catch golden hour (≈20 min before/after sun touches the horizon).

| # | Shot | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wide static of the horizon, sun low | 6–8s | Tripod or rest on a ledge. Let color do the work. |
| 2 | Slow pan across the sky's color band | 5s | Pan *slowly* — fast pans blur and look bad in frames. |
| 3 | Sun behind a silhouette (tree, rooftop, hill) | 5s | Adds depth and a clear focal point for the grader. |
| 4 | Tight shot of clouds catching color | 4s | Optional. Texture sells "sky at its best." |
| 5 | You looking out, back to camera, sky filling frame | 5s | Optional human beat — ties to "no screen between you and it." |

**Audio:** keep ambient (wind/birds). A quiet exhale or one line ("there it is") gives the
editor an audible anchor.

## 2. End of the Line — arrival at an unknown place 🚏

**Brief:** "Board a bus/train whose line you've never ridden, take it to the last stop, explore."
**Proof:** "Photograph wherever the line leaves you."

Deliberately open — *any* place reads as proof. Shoot wherever you actually end up; an
unfamiliar, slightly off-the-map spot looks best.

| # | Shot | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The last-stop sign / platform / "end of line" marker | 5s | Establishes the literal payoff of the quest. |
| 2 | Stepping off — feet to ground, or doors opening POV | 4s | Motion + arrival. Natural footstep/door audio. |
| 3 | Slow look around the new place (pan or walk-through) | 6–8s | The "explore" beat. Walk smoothly, phone braced. |
| 4 | One characterful detail (mural, old shopfront, water, view) | 5s | Gives the grader something distinct to latch onto. |
| 5 | You small in the wide frame of the new place | 5s | Optional. Sense of "I went somewhere." |

**Audio:** street/ambient sound is perfect here and survives the silence trim.

## 3. Plant What Outlives You — plant a tree 🌱

**Brief:** "Plant a tree. Prepare the soil, water it, choose a place it can grow for years."
**Proof:** "Photograph the tree the day you planted it, crooked and small."

A sapling from any nursery + a patch of soil (yard, pot, community plot). No face needed.

| # | Shot | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hands digging / preparing the soil | 5s | Process shot. Spade-in-dirt audio is great. |
| 2 | Lowering the sapling into the hole | 5s | The decisive action — clearest "planting" frame. |
| 3 | Patting soil + watering the base | 5s | Water sound + movement reads as care/effort. |
| 4 | **Hero shot: the planted sapling, framed, soil visible** | 6s | This is *the* proof frame — steady, well-lit, sapling centered. |
| 5 | Wide of the spot showing "room to grow" | 4s | Optional. Sells "a place it can grow for years." |

**Audio:** keep the digging/watering sounds — they're audible anchors the editor will keep.

## Demo-day flow (ties to DEMO_SCRIPT.md)

1. Have these clip sets on the phone **before** recording the screencast.
2. In the showcase, when the omen reveals one of these three quests, accept it.
3. On *Proof of Legend*, upload that quest's 3–5 clips, tap **Cook my proof**.
4. Watch the Forge stages, then the Verdict score — the clips above are built to land a
   strong number because they visibly match the brief and the pacing the editor wants.

**Tip:** Sunrise is the most reliable on-camera win — shoot it first as your safety quest.
